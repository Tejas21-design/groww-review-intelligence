# Groww weekly product pulse

**Review window:** 23 Jul–25 Sep 2026 · **Sample:** 8 public Reddit excerpts · **Method:** manual clustering; convenience sample, not representative.

## Top themes

1. **Sell proceeds and charge breakdown (5/8):** users ask why net sale proceeds are lower than the sale value and what “external charges” include.
2. **Charge visibility in reporting (2/8):** users say order summaries and contract notes do not make charge details easy to find.
3. **Brokerage rule clarity (1/8):** the ₹5 minimum versus ₹20 or 0.1% rule is unclear for small orders.

## User voice

- “How does calculating work in the Groww app? ... I sold 1 share for 35.88. ... leaving 29.69. What happened here?” — Aug 14
- “...about Rs.20 per executed order as brokerage ... Rs. 5 is minimum which I do not understand.” — Aug 24
- “...i sell the stocks for 324 and how does it only show 200 and what are these external charges.” — Sep 25

## Key observation

The strongest signal is confusion at sale: users cannot readily map the sale value to net proceeds and deductions. This is a small-sample signal, not a measured platform-wide trend.

## Product actions

1. Preview estimated net proceeds and itemized charges before sell confirmation.
2. Explain the ₹5 minimum / ₹20-or-0.1% brokerage rule with a small-order example.
3. Link completed orders to their contract-note breakdown and a plain-language charge glossary.

# Groww support snippet: charges when selling delivery shares

- The amount received after selling shares can be lower than the sale value because several charges may apply; the exact amount depends on the trade and exchange.
- Brokerage is Groww’s trading fee. For equity orders, Groww lists brokerage as the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% of the order value, subject to a ₹5 minimum.
- A DP (Depository Participant) charge applies when shares are debited from your demat account on a sale. Groww’s pricing page lists a depository component and a Groww component; the Groww component is waived when the debit value is below ₹100.
- Other applicable line items can include STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charge, stamp duty (buy side), IPFT, and GST on specified charges. Not every item applies to every side/type of trade.
- For the exact transaction-level breakdown, check the contract note and Groww’s charge/pricing details; order value and net proceeds are different amounts.
- This snippet explains common charge components and is not a calculation for a specific trade. Use Groww’s brokerage calculator for an estimate.
